PANTE Whitepaper

Abstract

PANTE is a meme token with utility, designed to bridge the gap between meme culture and decentralized finance (DeFi). Built on sustainable development principles, PANTE offers a comprehensive ecosystem including a DEX, liquidity mining, staking, and governance.

1. Introduction

The meme token space has been dominated by hype-driven projects with little to no utility. PANTE changes this narrative by introducing real DeFi utilities while maintaining the cultural relevance of meme tokens.

3. Ecosystem

3.1 PanteSwap (DEX)

Our decentralized exchange allows users to swap tokens with low fees and high liquidity. Built on AMM (Automated Market Maker) model.

3.2 Liquidity Mining

Users provide liquidity to trading pairs and earn LP tokens. Stake LP tokens to earn $PANTE rewards.

3.3 Staking

Stake your $PANTE tokens to earn passive income. Flexible and locked staking options with varying APY.

3.4 Governance

$PANTE holders can propose and vote on protocol upgrades, fee structures, and treasury allocations.
